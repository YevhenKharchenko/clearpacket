Get Really Good at Matching These Color Blocks

Clear Grid's pretty easy to learn, but don't be fooled - it gets challenging real quick. Here's how it works:

  • Drag and Merge:

    Match similar blocks by dragging them into the correct positions to create combinations.

  • Clear the Grid:

    When you match them right, they just disappear - super satisfying - and then more blocks drop down.

  • Level Up:

    Unlock new stages with more complex and creative block arrangements to solve.

  • The controls are dead simple - just drag stuff around - but I swear this game has some kind of spell on it because I always end up playing way longer than I planned.

  • Hundreds of challenging levels to keep you entertained
